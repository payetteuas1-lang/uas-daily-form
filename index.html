📲 How to Install as an App
iOS (Safari): Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome/Edge): Tap menu (⋮) → Install app or Add to Home screen.
1. LRZ Map Location Picker
• Tap 🗺️ Map next to the LRZ location box to open the interactive map.
• Click anywhere on the map or drag the pin to set the exact latitude/longitude.
• Tap 📍 Locate Me to zoom to your current position.
2. Military Time & Availability
• Enter Military Time (e.g. 0800 to 2200) for daily shift hours and sorties.
• 14 hours = 1.00 Day Availability. Scheduled shifts under 14 hours automatically calculate as a decimal fraction of daily availability.
3. Incident Package Manager
• Tap 📦 Package to batch print all selected days into a consolidated PDF, export CSVs, or transfer master JSON packages.
